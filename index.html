Privacy Policy for Cluster

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Cluster ("the app," "we," "our") is designed with your privacy as a core principle. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

Overview

Cluster does not have a server or backend. All photo and video processing happens entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to your photos, videos, or any data generated by the app.

Photo Library Access

Cluster requests access to your Photos library in order to:

  • Display your photos and videos within the app
  • Generate index data of each photo and video, which are used to power search and clustering features
  • Organize your photos and videos into Albums (created by you) and Clusters (created automatically based on similarity)

Data Storage

All data generated by the app — including index data, Clusters, and app settings — is stored locally on your device. This data is not transmitted off your device by Cluster.

Analytics and Tracking

Cluster does not use any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. We do not track your activity in the app, and no usage data is collected or transmitted to us or any third party.

In-App Purchases

Cluster offers an optional one-time purchase to unlock full search results. This purchase is processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. Cluster does not receive or store your payment information; this is handled directly by Apple in accordance with Apple's own privacy practices.

Third-Party Services

Cluster does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs beyond Apple's own system frameworks (Photos, SwiftData, StoreKit, CoreML).

Children's Privacy

Cluster does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, as the app does not collect or transmit personal data at all.

Your Choices

You can revoke Cluster's access to your Photos library at any time in your device's Settings app under Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos. If access is revoked, the app will no longer be able to index, search, or display your photos.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
